# PPTX Templates

## KP (Commercial Proposal) Structure

```
Slide 1: Title + company logo + date
Slide 2: Problem statement
Slide 3: Our solution (key features)
Slide 4: How it works (diagram/flow)
Slide 5: Technology stack
Slide 6: Timeline & milestones
Slide 7: Team
Slide 8: Pricing
Slide 9: Next steps / CTA
Slide 10: Contacts
```
